# Regulars — Screen-by-Screen Manual

Gym management software that tells you when your regulars stop being regular.

Written for the people who will actually use it — the owner and the front desk.
No technical knowledge assumed. Each section covers one screen: what it is,
what is on it, and what you can do there.

Screens appear in the order they sit on the dashboard.

# Login

**What it is** — the front door.

**On the screen:** phone number, password, Login.

**To use it:** enter the phone number and password you were given, tap
**Login**.

Works in any browser on a laptop, tablet or phone. Nothing to install.

**Give every person their own login.** The software records who took each
payment and who called which member. Share one account and that record becomes
worthless.

Two kinds of account:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including staff, plans, pricing and reports |
| **Staff** | Members, check-ins, payments, shop, At Risk |

# Dashboard

**What it is** — the first screen after login. It answers "what needs
attention today".

**On the screen, top to bottom:**

- **Your name and the greeting**, with the sign-out button
- **Alert bar** — members inactive 30+ days, renewals due within 7 days
- **Today's Overview** — total members, active members, expiring soon, revenue
  this month
- **Renewals card** — due today / this week / overdue
- **Revenue card** — this month / today / pending
- **Quick Actions** — the way into every screen below

**To use it:** tap any card to open the detail behind it. Nothing on the
dashboard is edited directly.

# 1. Members

**What it is** — every member of the gym. The screen you will use most.

**On the screen:** a search box, filters by status, and one card per member
showing name, phone, plan and expiry.

### Add a member

1. Tap **Add Member**
2. Fill in name, phone, plan, start date
3. Save

Expiry is worked out from the plan length. **Phone numbers must be unique** —
that is how somebody is found at the front desk.

### The member's page

Tap any member. Everything about one person in one place: plan and expiry,
check-in history, payments and invoices, PT package, shop purchases, wallet
balance, and a full history of changes.

### Actions on the member's page

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Renew** | Extends the membership and records the payment |
| **Freeze** | Pauses for injury, travel or illness |
| **Change plan** | Moves them to a different plan |
| **Transfer** | Moves them to another branch |
| **Terminate** | Ends the membership, with a reason |

Every action is recorded with the date and who did it. Nothing is silently
overwritten — if somebody asks in six months why their membership was frozen
in March, the answer is on their page.

# 2. Leads

**What it is** — enquiries who are not members yet.

**On the screen:** a pipeline board with a column per stage. Drag a lead
between columns as they progress.

**Also here:**

- **Follow-up queue** — who to contact today
- **Activity timeline** — every call and note against a lead
- **Assign** — give a lead to a specific staff member
- **Analytics** — where leads come from and how they convert

### Convert a lead

Open the lead → **Convert**. This creates the member and carries their details
across, so nothing is retyped.

# 3. Renewals

**What it is** — everyone whose membership is expiring, so nobody lapses
unnoticed.

**On the screen:** members grouped by urgency — due today, due this week,
overdue.

**To use it:** tap a member to renew them, or to call first. Renewing records
the payment and moves the expiry date forward.

# 4. At Risk

**What it is** — the retention worklist, and the screen that makes this
software different. **It is a worklist, not a report.** Every row is a member
and a phone call.

**To use it:** tap **Run scan** to update. Sections appear in urgency order.

### Handled this week

Who has been working the list, and which members they contacted. Hidden until
somebody has resolved something.

### First 90 days

More members are lost here than anywhere else — usually not because they were
unhappy, but because they never really started. Three groups, each a different
conversation:

- **Paid, never walked in** — joined and has not come once. The easiest member
  on the list to save, and it gets harder every day.
- **Started, then stopped** — came a few times, then went quiet.
- **Coming too rarely to stick** — under about 1.5 visits a week, which never
  becomes a habit.

Each card suggests what the call is for.

### Routine has broken

Members **still coming just as often** — but no longer at their usual time.

Somebody who trained at 7am every weekday and now turns up at 6am, then 9pm,
then Saturday afternoon has lost the habit that was holding them. Their visit
count has not dropped, so nothing else would flag them. Attendance usually
follows a few weeks later.

Each card shows the evidence: how often they kept their slot before (say
48 of 48 visits), how often now (2 of 15), and that their visits per week are
unchanged.

**What to do:** don't say "we miss you" — they haven't gone anywhere. Ask
whether their schedule has changed, then help them build a new fixed time: a
class, a trainer slot, two agreed days. You are re-anchoring a current member,
not chasing a lapsed one.

### The usual alerts

Expiring, lapsed, and members who have stopped coming — grouped by severity.

### Marking something handled

Tap the tick. You are asked what you did; the note is optional but is recorded
against your name.

This is the part that makes everything else worth having. A perfect alert
nobody rings is worth nothing.

# 5. Payments

**What it is** — money taken and money still owed.

**On the screen:** payment history, with pending dues highlighted.

### Collect a payment

1. **Collect payment**, or open the member and collect from there
2. Choose the amount and method — cash, UPI, card, bank transfer
3. Save

Recorded against whoever is logged in. You can raise an invoice from a payment
in one tap.

# 6. Shop

**What it is** — the counter. Supplements, drinks, accessories.

**Three tabs:**

### Sell

Build a basket, optionally pick a member, take payment. Cash, UPI, or the
member's **wallet** balance.

Wallet payments come out as part of the sale itself — if the sale fails,
nothing is deducted.

### Stock

Current levels, with low-stock warnings against your reorder level.

Stock is driven by recorded movements, never typed in, so the screen and the
shelf stay in step. A sale that would take stock below zero is refused rather
than allowed to go negative.

### Sales

History, including refunds.

# 7. Invoices

**What it is** — GST invoices with proper sequential numbering.

**On the screen:** every invoice with its status — draft, unpaid, partly paid,
paid.

**Two things worth understanding:**

- **Numbering is gapless, per financial year.** Numbers are assigned when an
  invoice is *issued*, not drafted, so there are never holes in the sequence.
- **An issued invoice cannot be edited.** If something is wrong, cancel it and
  issue a new one. That is what makes your books defensible.

Prices are **tax-inclusive** by default — the price you show a member is what
they pay, and GST is worked out from inside it. Changeable in billing settings.

Discounts can be applied per invoice line.

# 8. Attendance

**What it is** — check-ins, and the front desk's home screen.

**On the screen:** today's check-ins, recent history, and the **Check in**
button.

### Check somebody in

1. Tap **Check in**
2. Search by name or phone
3. Tap the member

A member can only be checked in once per day. Tapping twice does not create a
second visit.

### The counter prompt

Sometimes a short note appears after a check-in:

> **Back after a break** — *"First time back in 24 days. Say welcome back —
> don't ask where they've been."*

> **Due a restock** — *"Last bought Whey Protein 1kg 31 days ago — they buy
> about every 30."*

> **First ever visit** — *"Make sure someone shows them round and books their
> next session."*

**Most check-ins show nothing.** That is deliberate. If a note appeared every
time you would stop reading it within a week.

The note is **for you, not the member** — say it however fits, or not at all.
**Did it** records that you acted; **Not now** just closes. Neither is
compulsory.

You will never see anything about money owed here. That conversation belongs
somewhere private, not at a counter with other members in earshot.

# 9. Classes

**What it is** — the group timetable.

**To set up:** create a **class type** (Yoga, HIIT), then a **schedule** (day,
time, capacity, trainer). Schedules generate individual **sessions**.

**On a session:** who is booked, who is waitlisted, and attendance marking.

When a session is full, further bookings join a **waitlist** and move up
automatically when somebody cancels.

> **Note:** classes are included in membership. There is no per-class charging
> or class-pack selling.

# 10. Plans

**What it is** — your membership types and prices. Owner only.

**On the screen:** each plan with duration and price.

Create a plan with a name, length in days and price. Plans drive expiry dates
and renewal amounts everywhere else.

Changing a plan's price does not change what existing members already paid —
past invoices keep the price that was charged at the time.

# 11. Reports

**What it is** — the numbers, by period and by branch.

Revenue, member growth, attendance, retention. Use it for the month-end view;
use At Risk for today's work.

# 12. Staff

**What it is** — who can log in. Owner only.

Add a staff member with name, phone and role (owner or staff). They log in with
that phone and the password you set.

**Deactivate rather than delete** when somebody leaves — their record of who
did what stays intact.

# 13. Visitors

**What it is** — walk-ins, trial sessions and tours: people in the building
who are not members.

Record who came, why, and what happened. A visitor can be converted into a
member or a lead.

---

# 14. Referrals

**What it is** — members who brought other members in.

Record who referred whom and whether it converted. Useful for knowing which
members are worth thanking, and how much of your growth is word of mouth.

---

# 15. Branches

**What it is** — for gyms with more than one location. Skip it if you have one.

Each branch has its own members, staff and takings. Switch between them here.

**Also here:**

- **Transfer** members, staff and trainers between branches
- **Targets** — monthly revenue and member targets per branch
- **Chain overview** — branches compared

A member belongs to one branch at a time, and transfers are recorded.

# 16. Import data

**What it is** — bringing members, plans and payments from a spreadsheet or
your previous software.

**It works in two stages, and this matters:**

1. **Check** — the file is read and every row judged, but **nothing is saved**.
   You see exactly what would be imported, what is invalid and why, and what
   looks like a duplicate.
2. **Import** — only the rows that passed are saved.

Nothing is written until you have seen what will happen. If 40 rows are wrong,
fix those 40 and check again; the good rows are not lost.

**Dates must be day-first** — 31/01/2026 is 31 January. American month-first
dates are rejected rather than guessed at, because silently reading 01/02 as
January 2nd would corrupt every date in your file.

Column headings are matched flexibly, so "Phone", "Mobile" and "Contact No" are
all understood.

# 17. Trainers

**What it is** — your training staff and their specialisations.

Add a trainer with name, phone and specialisation. Trainers can be attached to
classes and to PT packages.

---

# 18. Personal Training

**What it is** — one-to-one training: packages and appointments.

**Sell a package:** member, trainer, number of sessions, price. An invoice can
be raised from it in one tap.

**Book an appointment:** member, trainer, date and time. Sessions are deducted
as they are used, and the remaining balance is always visible.

When a member is down to their last couple of sessions, the front desk sees a
note at check-in — the natural moment to talk about the next block.

# What this software does not do

Being straight about this matters more than a longer feature list.

- **It does not send messages.** No automatic SMS, WhatsApp or email to
  members. It tells a human who to contact; the human decides what to say.
  WhatsApp is planned, not built.
- **It does not charge for classes.** Classes are included in membership.
- **It does not connect to a biometric or turnstile device.** Check-ins are
  recorded in the app.
- **It does not take online payments from members.** It records payments you
  have taken.
- **There is no member-facing app.** Members do not log in; your staff do.
- **It does not predict who will quit, or score members out of 100.** Every
  flag can be traced back to that member's actual visits — which means you can
  always check whether it is right.

### One idea behind the retention screens

People leave a gym when the **habit** breaks, not when they decide to quit —
and the habit breaks weeks earlier.

That is what **First 90 days** and **Routine has broken** are looking for. Both
are arithmetic on your own check-in records: nothing invented, nothing guessed,
every flag traceable to real visits.

The one thing the software cannot do is make the phone call.
